Wear My Mirror

Each student animated something from their own life to wear on your face. Turn on your camera and try them on.

Your camera stays on your phone. Nothing is recorded or uploaded unless you take a photo and choose to save or share it.

Hold your phone at arm's length and look at the screen

The pieces

    About

    Wear My Mirror began as a smart mirror: a TV behind two-way glass, with a camera that finds each face and places an animation on its reflection. Black pixels stay mirror; only light shows through.

    Students in ART 104A Digital Media I finished one sentence, “Look into my mirror and you'll wear ___, because ___,” and animated the answer in Photoshop and After Effects. The meaning is theirs. The face is yours.

    Your privacy

    Face tracking runs on your phone. The camera image is never recorded or sent anywhere. A photo leaves your phone only if you save or share it.
